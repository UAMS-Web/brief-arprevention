Brief : AR Prevention is a child theme of the Genesis Framework for WordPress, based on the Brief theme by Minimal Genesis. This wiki covers setting the theme up, its Customizer settings, its page templates, what it changes in Genesis, and troubleshooting. The [README](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/brief-arprevention#readme) keeps the overview and the installation steps.

## Getting started

- [Installation (README)](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/brief-arprevention#readme): put the theme and its Genesis parent in place and activate it.
- [Setting up the theme](Setting-up-the-theme.md): from an activated theme to a site with menus, a logo and its colors.
